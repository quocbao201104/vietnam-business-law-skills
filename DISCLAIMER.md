# Disclaimer

Vietnam Business Law Skills is an open-source research and decision-support project.

It is **not a law firm**, does not provide legal representation, and does not create a lawyer-client or attorney-client relationship.

Outputs produced with this skill are not a substitute for professional legal advice tailored to the specific facts, documents, timing, jurisdiction, and risk of a real matter.

## Current law can change

The project is designed around the principle:

```text
Stable reasoning, live law.
```

It attempts to separate durable reasoning from time-sensitive legal rules, but no automated system can guarantee that every source, amendment, transition rule, administrative practice, or factual dependency has been identified or interpreted correctly.

Users should independently verify material legal propositions against current authoritative sources before taking action, especially where the decision is high-impact, irreversible, deadline-sensitive, regulated, disputed, or cross-border.

## Jurisdiction and scope

The project is primarily designed for Vietnamese business and commercial legal questions, with cross-border analysis where relevant.

A result that is useful for one transaction, date, actor, sector, or factual pattern may not apply to another.

## No guarantee of completeness or outcome

The project may produce incomplete, conditional, disputed, or incorrect conclusions. It may also identify that professional legal review is required.

Nothing in the repository or an output generated from it guarantees a legal, regulatory, tax, contractual, litigation, customs, employment, or business outcome.
